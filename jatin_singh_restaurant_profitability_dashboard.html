Key Insights & Recommendations
01

Protect Dine-in Margin

Dine-in has the highest margin at 25.9%. Improve table utilization, premium add-ons and upselling.

02

Shift Repeat Customers Direct

Own delivery generates 23.0% margin. Encourage repeat customers to order through the restaurant's direct channel.

03

Control Platform Leakage

Third-party platforms deliver only 13.2% margin. Negotiate commissions and reduce discount dependency.

04

Attack Food Cost

Food cost is the largest cost component at 31.6%. Focus on procurement, recipe standardization, portion control and waste.

05

Optimize Weak Menu Items

Low-margin items should be repriced, redesigned or removed after considering their strategic role in the menu.

06

Maintain Break-even Buffer

Revenue is ₹32.89 L above break-even, giving the business a 26.3% margin of safety.
